Sites

The slider only works on the sites listed here, and only once Chrome has granted access to them. Prime Video and Disney+ are on the list to begin with; both can be removed like any other site.

One exact host per entry: example.com does not cover www.example.com or video.example.com. The popup lists every host a page plays video from, so you can add the right one from there.

Listed sites

    Granted sites also appear under Site access on this extension’s card in chrome://extensions, where access can be revoked again; the site stays on this list, waiting to be granted. Pages that were already open when access was granted keep running without the slider until they are reloaded.
